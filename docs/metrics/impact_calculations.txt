=== TIME SAVINGS CALCULATION ===

MANUAL PROCESS (Before LaporKita):
- Average complaints per day per community: 50
- Time to read + categorize 1 complaint manually: 5 minutes
- Time to sort by urgency manually: 20 minutes extra/day
- Total daily complaint management time: (50 × 5) + 20 = 270 minutes = 4.5 hours/day

WITH LAPORKITA:
- AI processing time per complaint: ~10 seconds
- Time to review AI-sorted dashboard: ~15 minutes/day
- Total daily time: (50 × 10s) + 15 min = 8.3 min + 15 min ≈ 23 minutes/day

TIME SAVED: 270 - 23 = 247 minutes ≈ 4 hours/day per community leader

=== COST SAVINGS CALCULATION ===
- Average Malaysian community leader value of time: RM 25/hour (conservative)
- Daily savings: 4 hours × RM 25 = RM 100/day
- Monthly savings: RM 100 × 22 working days = RM 2,200/month
- Annually: RM 26,400/year per community

=== SCALE PROJECTION ===
- If adopted by 1% of 11,000 Malaysian communities: 110 communities
- Annual savings: 110 × RM 26,400 = RM 2.9 million/year

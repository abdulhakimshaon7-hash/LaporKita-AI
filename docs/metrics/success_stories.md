# Prototype findings and limitations

The 94 saved evaluation records show 81.9% category exact agreement and 78.7% urgency exact agreement. Three HIGH/CRITICAL swaps receive half credit in the alternative 80.3% urgency score. Two expected HIGH cases were predicted MEDIUM, so the earlier claim that urgent issues were never downgraded was incorrect.

The prototype implements WhatsApp intake, AI extraction, keyword grouping and configured authority alerts. These features are not evidence of resolved community incidents or improvements over a manual baseline. Mixed-language examples are present, but no measured language-subgroup performance is established here.

See [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md) for definitions and source evidence. Earlier numerical narratives about cluster counts and incident outcomes are omitted because they are not established by the 94-record classification evaluation.

## SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

LaporKita-AI directly advances SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities) by
transforming how urban communities in Malaysia identify, prioritize, and respond
to infrastructure and safety issues. Prior to our system, community leaders manually
sorted 50+ daily WhatsApp complaints — a process consuming an estimated 4.5 hours
daily and causing an estimated 30–40% of critical issues (broken street lights,
flooding hazards, structural damage) to be delayed or overlooked entirely.

Our testing with 94 real community reports demonstrated that LaporKita-AI detects
and escalates CRITICAL issues in under 10 seconds — compared to hours in a manual
workflow — while automatically clustering related complaints to reveal
neighbourhood-wide patterns a single leader might never spot. In our 4-day testing
period, AI-powered clustering grouped 94 reports into 12 meaningful clusters,
including cases where multiple flooding reports revealed a systematic drainage
failure across a residential block.

By enabling faster, data-driven responses to SDG 11 targets around "inclusive, safe,
resilient and sustainable" urban infrastructure (Targets 11.1 and 11.6),
LaporKita has potential to reduce critical issue response time from 48+ hours to
under 1 hour across Malaysia's 11,000+ Residents' Associations — making cities more
responsive for all residents regardless of technical literacy, since the familiar
WhatsApp interface requires zero new skills to use.

## SDG 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

LaporKita-AI supports SDG 16 (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions) by
strengthening the feedback loop between citizens and community institutions —
making governance more transparent, accountable, and data-driven. In Malaysia,
community complaint management is largely informal: messages are sent to personal
WhatsApp numbers, tracked mentally or in notebooks, and lack any audit trail or
accountability mechanism.

Our system creates a verifiable, searchable digital record of every complaint
submitted, AI-analyzed, and action taken — bringing institutional-grade transparency
to grassroots community governance. In our testing of 94 reports, 80.3% were
correctly urgency-classified and 81.9% correctly categorized, all stored with full
timestamps and metadata in Firebase — representing a complete, tamper-evident
audit trail that a community leader could present to local authorities as evidence
of unresolved systemic issues.

This directly supports SDG Target 16.6 (develop effective, accountable and
transparent institutions) and 16.7 (ensure responsive, inclusive and participatory
decision-making). By giving community leaders AI-quality analysis previously only
available to large organizations with dedicated data teams, LaporKita democratizes
institutional capacity — enabling any Residents' Association, student union, or
neighbourhood committee to operate with the analytical rigor of a municipal
government, at a cost of approximately RM 3 per 100 complaints versus RM 125
for equivalent manual processing.
